# Alpha Particle Spectrometry with a Semiconductor Detector

## Overview
This experiment investigated alpha-particle energy spectra and energy loss in matter using an Americium-241 source and a silicon surface-barrier detector. Experimental measurements were compared with SRIM simulations.

## Focus
- Alpha-particle spectroscopy using semiconductor detectors
- Energy calibration and detector resolution
- Alpha-particle stopping power and range in air
- Energy loss and straggling in Mylar absorbers
- Comparison of experimental results with SRIM simulations

## Key Results
- Detector energy resolution: 0.64%
- Experimental alpha-particle range in air: (41.02 ± 0.22) mm
- Predicted range in air: 40.86 mm
- Estimated range in Mylar: 31.50 µm
- Mylar range deviation from SRIM: 3.81%

## Summary
The experiment demonstrated the use of semiconductor detectors for charged-particle spectroscopy and investigated how alpha particles lose energy in different absorbing materials. Experimental results showed good agreement with stopping-range predictions and SRIM simulations.
