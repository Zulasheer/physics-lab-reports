# Physics Laboratory Reports

**Experimental Physics | Scientific Computing | Data Analysis**

A collection of 17 experimental physics laboratory investigations conducted during my BSc Physics studies in the International Physics Studies Program (IPSP) at Leipzig University.

This repository documents my practical experience in experimental methods, instrumentation, data acquisition, numerical analysis, theoretical modelling, and scientific reporting.

The experiments cover a broad range of physics, from classical optics and electronic circuits to semiconductor physics, spectroscopy, crystallography, and magnetic resonance.

---

## Repository Overview

The repository is divided into two sections:

- **Experimental Physics Laboratory:** 11 experiments covering classical optics, electric circuits, semiconductor devices, and electron microscopy.
- **Advanced Physics Laboratory:** 6 experiments covering spectroscopy, nuclear and magnetic resonance, semiconductor detectors, and structural characterization of materials.

Each experiment has a dedicated folder containing its final laboratory report and an experiment-specific README. Selected experiments also include supporting measurement data, Jupyter notebooks, or analysis files.

## Repository Structure

Each experiment is organized in its own folder. Depending on the experiment and the available files, the structure includes:

- `README.md` — Experiment overview, methods, and key results
- PDF laboratory report — Theoretical background, experimental methods, results and discussion
- Supporting data — Original or processed experimental measurements, where available
- Analysis files — Jupyter notebooks or other computational analysis files, where available

Individual experiment READMEs provide additional information about the methods and findings.
