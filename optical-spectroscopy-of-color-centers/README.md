# Optical Spectroscopy of Colour Centres and Molecules

## Overview
This experiment investigated molecular iodine absorption spectra and optical colour centres in alkali halide crystals using UV/VIS spectroscopy, with particular emphasis on the Franck–Condon principle and electronic-vibrational transitions.

## Focus
- UV/VIS spectrometer calibration using holmium oxide
- Optical transmission and absorption spectroscopy
- Molecular iodine vibrational spectra
- Franck–Condon principle and molecular dissociation energies
- F-centre formation in KBr and NaCl crystals
- Comparison of classical and quantum-mechanical F-centre models

## Key Results
- Average relative wavelength calibration error: approximately 0.39%
- Iodine electronic transition energy: (1.9389 ± 0.0003) eV
- Iodine ground-state dissociation energy: 1.5547 eV
- KBr F-centre absorption maximum: approximately 640 nm
- NaCl F-centre absorption maximum: 473.65 nm

## Summary
The experiment connected molecular spectroscopy with solid-state defect physics through optical absorption measurements. Analysis of iodine vibrational transitions and F-centres in irradiated alkali halides provided insight into electronic transitions, lattice interactions, and defect-state models.
