# Pulsed Nuclear Magnetic Resonance in Liquids

## Overview
This experiment investigated proton nuclear magnetic resonance in liquid samples using a MARAN Ultra spectrometer and the RiNMR software environment.

## Focus
- Calibration of 90° and 180° radio-frequency pulses
- Longitudinal relaxation time (T1) measurements
- Free-induction decay (FID) and Fourier transformation
- Frequency-domain analysis and chemical-shift uncertainty
- Hahn echo and CPMG pulse sequences
- Transverse relaxation time (T2) measurements

## Key Results
- 90° pulse length: 2.85 µs
- 180° pulse length: 5.70 µs
- Longitudinal relaxation time of water: 332.522 ms
- Hahn-echo transverse relaxation time of water: (65.82 ± 0.60) ms
- CPMG transverse relaxation time of water: approximately 70.8 ms

## Summary
The experiment investigated fundamental NMR phenomena through radio-frequency pulse calibration, relaxation measurements, Fourier analysis of free-induction decay signals, and spin-echo techniques. It demonstrated how pulse sequences can be used to characterize nuclear spin dynamics in liquids.
